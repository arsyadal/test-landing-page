# Chronicle — High-Precision Signal & Time-Series Engine

## Product Truth
Chronicle is an industrial-grade telemetry and time-series observability engine designed for infrastructure engineers and hardware systems architects. It captures, indices, and visualizes high-frequency telemetry streams at microsecond resolution without sampling loss.

## Target Audience & Operational Context
- Infrastructure engineers, systems programmers, aerospace/industrial telemetry operators.
- Usage scene: High-density monitoring stations, tactical war rooms, engineering terminals under focused ambient lighting.

## Core Value Proposition
- Deterministic ingestion: Zero-loss ingestion pipeline handling up to 4,000,000 continuous signal points per node per second.
- Pure analytical precision: Every measurement represents empirical instrument truth. No synthetic smoothing, no decorative approximations.

## Non-Goals
- Chronicle is not a generic consumer analytics dashboard. It avoids fluffy vanity metrics, decorative gradient cards, and artificial engagement gamification.
